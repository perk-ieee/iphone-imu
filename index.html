Mobile browsers don't expose a phone's raw magnetometer to web pages, so the third triad shown here is fused orientation (compass heading, tilt, roll) rather than raw magnetic field — the closest 3-axis substitute available in-browser, on iPhone or Android. Location comes from the device's GPS/location services and is requested separately from motion access. The two temperature readings are weather estimates for your GPS coordinates from Open-Meteo and the US National Weather Service (NWS, US locations only) — not a physical sensor on the phone — refreshed every few minutes or when you move more than ~2 km. Works on any modern phone browser; sensor and permission behavior is detected automatically.
